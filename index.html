About

I'm a computer engineering student at Singapore Polytechnic, generally curious about how things work. My interests sit around robotics, RF and electrical engineering, and software development, and I'm always trying to pick up something new. Lately that's meant sensor fusion for robot navigation and getting more comfortable with applied machine learning.

Education

I'm a Year 2 Computer Engineering student at Singapore Polytechnic. In AY27/28 Semester 2, I'll also spend a semester at NUS as a non-graduating student, taking Electrical & Computer Engineering modules through SP's University Accelerated Pathway Programme (UAPP).

Amateur Radio

A couple of years ago I got licensed as an amateur radio operator (9V1KA in Singapore, KF0RKQ in the US), and I've been slowly exploring what that opens up since: small PCBs, antennas, and writing software to decode signals by hand rather than relying on what ships with the radio.

Outside of that, I read poetry and try to get out for a run when I can.

Volunteering & Community

I spend a fair amount of time on programmes that try to get more students into tech, alongside a small food-aid project a few friends and I started.

  • YouthTechSG: I help run scholarship and open-source fellowship programmes that connect students with industry training.
  • SourceFoods: I co-founded a small platform that helps food-aid groups track donations instead of relying on paper logs.
  • SP Infocomm Club & SP Outstanding Talent: I help organise tech workshops and leadership pathways for other students.
