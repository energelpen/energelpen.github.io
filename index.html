About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Singapore Polytechnic and a Student Research Assistant at its 5G & AIoT Centre. My current work covers image data preparation, computer vision model training and evaluation, quantisation for edge devices, and embedded software for a low-cost space situational awareness payload.

I also work on autonomous robot navigation at the Advanced Robotics and Automation Centre (ARAC), where I've applied LiDAR, ultrasonic sensing, and ROS 2 to detect transparent obstacles.
